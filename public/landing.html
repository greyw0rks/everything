A few things to know.

Does “market value added” mean new money entered crypto?

No. Market capitalization measures valuation: price multiplied by circulating supply. A valuation change is different from net cash flowing into the market.

What do liquidation totals count?

The notional value of leveraged positions forcibly closed, with long and short breakdowns. Rolling 1h, 4h and 24h windows overlap.

How do extension notifications work?

Sign in and enable extension notifications from your account. Give the browser permission when asked. New alerts are checked every five minutes while the browser is running; the extension popup can stay closed.

Can I track crypto reports on X?

X tracking is a paid feature coming soon. Pricing is unset and subscriptions are not available yet.
